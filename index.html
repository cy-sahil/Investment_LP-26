How we worked this out+
  • Each plan starts with a share of your monthly income (10%, 20% or 30%) set aside every month.
  • If you choose a yearly increase, that monthly amount goes up by 5% or 10% once a year.
  • Your savings are grown at 8% a year (shorter end of the range) and 4% a year (longer end), while your goal's cost keeps rising (8% a year for education, 6% for the rest). The range shows when your savings first catch up with the cost.
  • Taxes and product charges aren't included. This is an educational estimate, not investment advice or a recommendation of any product.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this investment advice?+

No. It's an educational planning tool that estimates how long it could take to reach the goal you enter. It doesn't recommend any fund or product.

Why is the time shown as a range?+

They are the two standard illustrative growth rates used for insurance plans, so your time plan is shown as a range between them. Neither is a promise of returns. Real outcomes depend on where your money is kept, its charges and the market.

Can I use my own numbers?+

Yes. The starting cost is only an example. Type in your own figure, and choose whether to raise your savings each year.

Why does the cost go up?+

Prices rise every year. A clinic or a college seat will cost more by the time you need it, so the planner adds a yearly price rise (8% for education, 6% for the rest).

Will someone call me?+

Only if you agree. CoverYou calls come only from verified numbers, and you can check any caller on our Trust Center.
